# Project Hub

Project Hub is a simple, all-in-one web portal designed to launch lightweight study tools and digital utilities directly inside your web browser. It gives you instant, clutter-free access to your exam notes, revision files, and stage recitation texts without downloading extra apps or managing scattered local files.

---
## 1. How to Use

1. **Visit the Portal:** Open the live link in any browser:
   ` https://bgxumair.github.io/Project-Hub/ `
2. **Launch an App:** Click **Launch App** on any project card (such as ExamVault or Naat Book) to open it immediately.
3. **Use the Tools:**
   * **ExamVault:** Create folders for your subjects (e.g., Class Tests, Revision Work) and drag-and-drop your PDFs or image notes directly into them.
   * **Naat Book:** Access your saved Naat lyrics instantly in a clean, distraction-free reader without waiting for pages to load.
4. **Install on Your Phone for Instant Stage/Study Access:**
   * **iPhone (Safari):** Tap the **Share** button → select **Add to Home Screen**.
   * **Android (Chrome):** Tap the **Three Dots Menu** → select **Add to Home screen**.

---

## 2. Why I Made It
**ExamVault!**
* **For Exam Preparation:** Keeping track of study notes, test papers, and PDFs across different devices was frustrating. Files got buried in downloads folders, local links broke, and managing everything took extra time when I just needed to focus and study.

**NaatBook!**
* **For Stage Recitations:** When reciting Naats live on stage, finding the right lyrics was always stressful. Screenshots got buried deep inside crowded photo albums, and online websites were full of ads or need internet to load and show content.

I built Project Hub to solve these exact problems. It provides a permanent, clean online sanctuary where tools like **ExamVault** and **Naat Book** live side-by-side—giving you instant, reliable access on any phone or laptop right when you need it most.

---

## 3. What It's Used For

* **Centralized Study Management:** Organize class notes, unit test papers, and revision files neatly inside **ExamVault**.
* **Stage-Ready Recitation:** Perform on stage with **Naat Book** without worrying about lost screenshots, slow websites, or weak internet connection.
* **Study & Recite Anywhere:** Access your entire library seamlessly across phones, tablets, school computers, or laptops.
* **Low-Light & Late-Night Comfort:** Designed with soft dark-mode tones (`#2E2B2B` graphite and `#388186` teal) to eliminate screen glare during long study sessions and stage performances.
* **Version History:** Access previous builds and updates through the built-in version log whenever tools are upgraded.
