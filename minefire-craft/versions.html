MineFire Craft

Version history. Only one version has been released so far.

Version 1.0 Latest

  • Voxel battle royale with 20 players: you against 19 AI bots.
  • Two 200x200 maps: Block Bermuda and Desert Purgatory.
  • Plane drop with parachute, airdrops, loot crates and a shrinking blue zone.
  • Guns (AK-47, UMP, MP5, MP40), tools, mining, and instant walls, ramps and blocks.
  • Game modes: Classic and Training (Ranked is coming soon).
  • Three camera views on F5, crouch and sprint, custom keys on desktop and custom controls on mobile.
  • Achievements, a first-time tutorial, and sound effects with a Booyah victory animation.
